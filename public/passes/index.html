    What to look for

      Passes

      Set a location to see passes.

      A pass counts as visible when the object is sunlit while your sky is at least in civil twilight (Sun 6° below the horizon) and the object is above the horizon. Brightness is estimated only where the object's intrinsic magnitude is known. Predictions use public orbital elements and are usually right to within a minute for the next few days; low objects and Starlink satellites that are still raising their orbits can drift more. Weather isn't considered.
