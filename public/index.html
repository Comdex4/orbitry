Accuracy

Real data, with its limits stated

Computed, not guessed

Satellite positions come from published orbital elements run through SGP4, the standard model made for them. Good to a few kilometres when fresh, for visualising and timing passes, not for collision avoidance.

Data age on every view

Every object shows how old its orbital data is and roughly how far off its position may be. Records still awaiting review are labelled.

Every source credited

Each fact, photo and model links to where it came from, with its licence. Unlicensed images are left out, not guessed at.
