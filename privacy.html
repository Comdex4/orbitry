Privacy

Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version

Orbitry has no accounts and no advertising. The only personal information we collect is the email address you choose to give the waitlist.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist, your email address is stored with our email provider (Buttondown) and used only to tell you about Orbitry launches. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to delete your address at any time.

What runs in your browser

The globe calculates satellite positions locally in your browser. The site stores one cached copy of public orbital data in your browser's local storage so it doesn't have to re-download it on every visit. It contains no personal information, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Hosting and third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse. The page loads code libraries from public CDNs (cdnjs, jsDelivr and unpkg), fonts from Google Fonts, and orbital data from CelesTrak, so those services can see your IP address as part of normal requests. We currently run no analytics or tracking cookies.
